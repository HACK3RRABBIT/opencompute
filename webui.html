Masscan (Internet-wide sweep)
Raw-socket scan of the whole IPv4 space for open Ollama ports. Long-lived and resumable — stopping saves a resume point (paused.conf), starting continues where it left off. Uses almost no bandwidth (~0.5 Mbps at 2000 pps).
